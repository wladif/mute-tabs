# Mute Tabs

### Summary
Automatically mute selected websites by adding them to a customizable domain blacklist.


### Desciption
A lightweight browser extension that automatically mutes tabs on websites you choose. Add domains to a personal blacklist, and the addon instantly silences audio whenever you visit those sites — perfect for blocking autoplay videos, noisy ads, or distracting platforms without muting your entire browser. Simple, fast, and fully customizable.
